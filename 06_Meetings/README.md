# Meeting Notes & Working Decisions

> **Authority Level:** Category D (MEETING / WORKING DECISION)  
> Records verbal discussions, team agreements, and supervisor feedback. Working decisions recorded here remain non-normative until formalized into [[Decision-Registry]] or an ADR in `02_Decisions/`.

---

```markdown
## YYYY-MM-DD: [Meeting Title]
- **Attendees:** ...
- **Agenda:** ...
- **Key Discussion Points:** ...
- **Working Decisions:** ...
- **Action Items:**
  - [ ] [Task] (Owner, Deadline)
```
