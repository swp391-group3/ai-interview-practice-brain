### Key Observations Derived Exclusively from SQL:

1. **Strict 1:0..1 Relationship**: `performance_reports.session_id` carries a `UNIQUE` constraint in addition to its `FOREIGN KEY` reference. Therefore, an interview session may have zero or one report, but never multiple reports.
2. **Nullable Parents**: `interview_sessions.jd_id` and `interview_sessions.avatar_id` do not declare `NOT NULL` and use `ON DELETE SET NULL`. This permits mock sessions created without a job description or without an avatar profile.
3. **Many-to-Many Bridge**: `job_description_skills` functions as a bridge table between `job_descriptions` and `skills`, carrying its own surrogate UUID PK (`id`) alongside the composite `UNIQUE (job_description_id, skill_id)`.
4. **No Direct User FK on Performance Reports**: `performance_reports` relies solely on `session_id` to reach the session and candidate account (`session.user_id`).
